Ugh! English Privacy Policy

Ugh! English, by Great Atlantic Learning Games · Effective November 21, 2026

Ugh! English is a spelling and phonics word game. We built it to be safe for classrooms and for learners of all ages. The short version: we do not collect your personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Ugh! has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal data.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (your daily puzzle, your streak and your statistics) in storage on your own device. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it.

Challenge codes

Practice rounds have a challenge code you can share with a friend. The code is only a short string that selects a word. It contains no personal information and is not sent to us.

Third parties

Ugh! does not include third-party advertising, analytics or social-media tools, and it does not share data with anyone. If you download the app through Apple's App Store or TestFlight, Apple may collect information about your download or crashes under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. Teachers and parents can use Ugh! without creating accounts or giving consent for data collection.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
