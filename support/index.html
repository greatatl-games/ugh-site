Common questions

A real word was not accepted. Email us the word. We add words regularly.

I am stuck. Tap "Stuck? Take a break or see the answer" under the board. You can see the answer and play a new word with the same rule.

How do I practice a particular rule? Tap the dice icon at the top and choose any of the spelling rules.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Is it safe for students? Ugh! has no ads, accounts or data collection. See our privacy policy.
